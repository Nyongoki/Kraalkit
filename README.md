# Kraalkit — AI-Powered Livestock Health & Genetic Intelligence

> **Next-Generation Veterinary Triage, Genetic Traceability, Indigenous Lexicon & Outbreak Surveillance for Smallholder Livestock Farming in Africa.**

Kraalkit is a digital livestock health and breeding intelligence platform purpose-built for Kenya and emerging markets. It bridges the critical veterinary deficit for smallholder farmers and pastoralists by combining **multilingual symptom triage**, **strict-guardrail veterinary clinical AI (Vet Co-Pilot)**, **veterinarian-driven indigenous dialect lexicon crowdsourcing**, **computer vision semen straw authentication**, **epizootic outbreak surveillance mapping**, and **verified KVB veterinary geolocation**.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Pitch Deck Highlights

* **The Problem:** 
  Smallholder farmers produce **over 80% of Kenya's milk and meat**, yet lose up to **30–40% of their herds annually** to preventable diseases, delayed clinical triage, language barriers between farmers and clinicians, counterfeit breeding genetics, and an extreme shortage of field veterinarians in rural wards (1 vet per 15,000+ animals).
* **The Solution:** 
  An accessible, mobile-first intelligence platform where:
  1. Farmers report animal symptoms in **their own indigenous dialects** (*Ekegusii, Gĩkũyũ, Dholuo, Kalenjin, Oluluhyia, Maa, Kikamba, Somali, Turkana, Borana, Meru, Swahili*) or English.
  2. Field veterinarians act as **natural native lexicon contributors**, translating boma-level vernacular into confirmed clinical symptom entities with KVB certification.
  3. A deterministic clinical engine ranks diseases while an **enterprise AI Vet Co-Pilot ("Code computes, AI explains")** explains vetted supportive steps and formulates questions for visiting clinicians.
  4. Artificial Insemination (AI) straws are **scanned and audited** via computer vision/OCR to eliminate stud fraud, repeat services, and accidental inbreeding.
  5. Real-time **epizootic outbreak heatmaps** track disease spread radiuses with itemized species casualty breakdowns (Cattle, Poultry, Goats, Sheep) to alert County Veterinary Officers (SCVO).
  6. Farmers connect directly to certified Kenya Veterinary Board (KVB) veterinarians via **live GPS routing and encrypted WhatsApp case sharing**.

## 🏛️ Core Platform Pillars

### 1. 🩺 Clinical Symptom Triage & Multi-Dialect Mapping
* **Single Animal & Herd Outbreak Modes:** Evaluates health presentations across dairy cattle, goats, sheep, and poultry.
* **Streamlined Single-Column Intake:** Optimized for laptops, tablets, and mobile devices—Step 1 (Animal), Step 2 (Symptoms), and Step 3 (Location) lead smoothly into the bottom summary and evaluate action.
* **Dual Access Bottom Action:** Floating bottom action bar ensures farmers and field officers can submit and evaluate symptoms at any point without scrolling.
* **Multi-Dialect Search:** Farmers search and log signs in Kiswahili, Ekegusii, Gĩkũyũ, Kalenjin, Oluluhyia, Dholuo, Maa, Somali, and English.
* **County-Level Geocoding:** Auto-detects GPS coordinates or matches against Kenya's 47 counties to map disease incidence.

### 2. 🌿 Indigenous Veterinary Lexicon Hub (Vets as Natural Contributors)
* **The Veterinarian Advantage:** Practicing field veterinarians and animal health technicians interact on-farm with smallholders and pastoralists daily in their mother tongues. When a farmer describes illness with local words (*Omwiko, Kuhara damu, Loolo, Chebwekwek, Kuvimba matiti*), the attending veterinarian directly connects that colloquial descriptor to the precise clinical pathology.
* **Instant KVB Field Certification:** Terms contributed by registered veterinarians are pre-verified, immediately confirmed into the live dictionary, and stamped with the practitioner's credentials (e.g. `Dr. Timothy M., KVB Registered Vet • Kisii County`).
* **Clinical Field Context & Dialect Nuances:** Vets record *how* farmers in their county use the word (e.g. *"Spoken in Bomet when describing rapid drops in milk yield during early acute mastitis"*), indexing sub-dialects across Kenya's 13 indigenous languages (*Ekegusii, Gĩkũyũ, Dholuo, Kalenjin, Oluluhyia, Maa, Kikamba, Af-Soomaali, Ng'aturkana, Afaan Oromoo, Kĩmĩrũ, Kiswahili*).
* **Peer Verification Queue:** Licensed veterinarians can review, validate, and endorse community submissions from local livestock keepers before terms enter national triage search.

### 3. ✨ Kraalkit Vet Co-Pilot ("Code Computes, AI Explains")
* **Authentication-Gated Clinical Access:** Access to Vet Co-Pilot is strictly restricted to authenticated farmers and registered veterinary practitioners to maintain clinical accountability, prevent unprescribed drug misuse, and ensure traceability.
* **Deterministic Clinical Algorithm:** Disease rankings and contagion correlations are calculated deterministically against vetted veterinary medical rules—never hallucinated by generative AI.
* **RAX-4.0 AI Clinical Model:** Explains *why* symptoms match, analyzes herd mortality ratios, and creates 3 targeted clinical questions for the farmer to present to a visiting vet.
* **Strict KVB Guardrails:**
  * **Zero Hallucinated Prescriptions:** Strictly bans drug dosage units (`mg`, `ml`, `cc`), antibiotic names, and home remedies.
  * **Vetted Database First-Aid:** On-farm actions come verbatim from Kenya Veterinary Board approved biosecurity steps (rendered with 0ms delay).
  * **Confidence Calibration:** Explicitly states pattern correlation confidence percentages and clarifies that pattern checks are preliminary, not definitive diagnoses.
  * **Notifiable Disease Alerts:** Instant notification to alert Sub-County Veterinary Officers (SCVO) for notifiable epizootics (ECF, FMD, Lumpy Skin Disease, Anthrax, Blackleg).
  * **WhatsApp Case Sharing:** Generates an encrypted clinical case summary with reference ID for 1-click sharing to local veterinary officers.

### 4. 🧬 AI Semen Straw Verifier & Breeding Ledger
* **Straw Decoder Engine:** Scans and interprets 5-part alphanumeric cryogenic semen straw codes (KAGRC, ABS Global, World Wide Sires, Semex, Alta Genetics).
* **Pedigree Sire Verification:** Extracts Bull ID, NAAB code, breed (*Friesian, Ayrshire, Jersey, Guernsey, Sahiwal, Boran*), stud station, production/cryo date, batch lot, and sexed vs. conventional technology.
* **AI Infill & LLM Heuristic Fallback:** When printed straw ink is rubbed off or faded inside liquid nitrogen tanks, the AI model reconstructs missing pedigree tokens from national sire registries.
* **Farm Breeding Ledger:** Tracks cows served, service dates, expected return dates (18–24 days), projected calving dates (280 days), and repeat service flags.

### 5. 🗺️ Epizootic Outbreak Surveillance & Biosecurity GIS
* **Real-Time Hotspot Mapping:** Aggregates farmer case reports into county-level disease clusters with adjustable 7, 14, and 30-day time horizons.
* **Containment Radius Calculation:**
  * **Focal Epicenter:** 4 km to 16 km immediate outbreak zone.
  * **Outer Biosecurity Cordon:** 15 km to 45 km quarantine buffer perimeter alerting farmers and market authorities against animal movements.
* **Detailed Species-by-Species Breakdown:** Hotspot dialogue popups and details panels show exact breakdowns by animal type:
  * 🐄 **Dairy Cattle:** Number sick and dead
  * 🐔 **Poultry:** Number sick and dead
  * 🐐 **Goats:** Number sick and dead
  * 🐑 **Sheep:** Number sick and dead
* **Species Zone Filters:** Toggle views between All Epizootics, Cattle (FMD, Anthrax, ECF, LSD), Goats (CCPP, PPR), and Poultry (Newcastle, Gumboro).

### 6. 🛡️ KVB Registered Veterinarian Geolocation Directory
* **Certified Professional Directory:** Live registry of Kenya Veterinary Board certified veterinary doctors, clinics, and animal health technicians.
* **Haversine Distance & Route Calculation:** Matches farmers to the closest registered vet based on live GPS coordinates and animal species treated.
* **Direct Outreach:** 1-tap phone dialer and WhatsApp case summary handoff.

## 🚀 Quick Start & Local Deployment

### 1. Requirements
* Node.js ≥ 18
* MySQL 5.7+ / 8.0+

### 2. Setup & Installation
```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone <repo-url>
cd kraalkit

# Install dependencies (root & frontend)
npm install
npm --prefix frontend install

# Environment variables
cp .env.example .env
# Ensure DATABASE_URL and RAX_API_KEY / GEMINI_API_KEY are configured in .env

# Database migrations & seed data
npm run db:generate
npm run db:push
npm run db:seed

# Production build
npm run build
npm --prefix frontend run build

# Start server (serves API and built frontend on port 3000)
node dist/index.js
```

## 📜 Compliance & Disclaimers

* **KVB Advisory Compliance:** Kraalkit automated triage suggestions and AI explanations are preliminary clinical possibilities, not definitive medical diagnoses.
* **Notifiable Disease Regulation:** Epizootic diseases (such as Foot and Mouth Disease, Anthrax, Blackleg, and East Coast Fever) are reportable under Kenyan law to the Sub-County Veterinary Officer (SCVO).
