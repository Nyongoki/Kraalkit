================================================================================
SECTION 3: QUICK CHEAT-SHEET FOR THE PRESENTER
================================================================================
* What is NAAB code?
  The National Association of Animal Breeders code (e.g. 29 = ABS Global, 7 = Select Sires, 200 = Semex).
* What is CSS?
  Certified Semen Services — international sanitary standard guaranteeing semen is free of
  Campylobacter fetus, Trichomonas foetus, Leptospirosis, and BVD virus.
* What is Julian Date?
  YYDDD format stamped on straws (e.g. 24185 = Year 2024, 185th day = July 3rd, 2024).
* What is Sexcel vs Ultra 4M?
  Sexcel is ABS Global's proprietary sexing technology (CD-laser sorting). Ultra 4M is
  STgenetics / World Wide Sires high-concentration 4-million cell technology. Both deliver ~90% female calves.
